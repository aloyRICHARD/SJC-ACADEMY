Overview

Why this course exists

This course explores the development, implementation, and impact of arts-based interventions in healthcare settings. It equips healthcare professionals, artists, and therapists to integrate the arts into patient care — covering theoretical foundations, practical applications, and evaluation methods.

By the end

Learning outcomes

Theory & evidence

Understand the theoretical foundations and evidence base for arts-based interventions in healthcare.

Cross-disciplinary skills

Develop practical skills across visual, performing, literary, and music arts interventions.

Program design

Create integrative, multidisciplinary arts-based programs tailored to specific healthcare settings.

Evaluation

Evaluate the impact of arts-based interventions using qualitative and quantitative methods.

Applied practice

Design and implement effective interventions that promote healing and well-being in real settings.
