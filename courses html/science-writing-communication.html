Overview

Course overview

This course aims to develop the skills necessary for effective science writing and communication. It focuses on writing for different audiences, crafting compelling narratives, and utilizing various media to convey scientific information. Students will engage in practical writing exercises, peer reviews, and analysis of exemplary science communication.

Course flow

How the 10 modules progress

The course moves from writing fundamentals, into audience awareness, academic and public writing, multimedia and storytelling, then ethics, peer review, and editing — closing with a capstone project.

By the end

Learning outcomes

Develop clear and effective science writing skills tailored to different audiences.
Understand the principles of engaging and accurate science communication.
Create compelling narratives and utilize multimedia tools to enhance communication.
Navigate ethical considerations and ensure the integrity of science writing.
Gain experience in peer review and editing to improve the quality of science communication.
Assessment

How you'll be evaluated

Quizzes

Regular quizzes to assess understanding of key concepts.

Assignments

Practical writing assignments applying theoretical knowledge.

Discussions

Forum participation to encourage peer learning and engagement.

Peer reviews

Involvement in peer review exercises to improve writing and critical analysis.

Capstone project

A final project integrating course concepts into a comprehensive science communication piece.

Who it's for

The ideal student

Someone with a keen interest in science and a passion for writing — science students and professionals looking to improve their communication skills, journalists and writers specializing in science topics, educators, policy makers, and anyone interested in making scientific information accessible and engaging.

Academic interests & goals

  • Science enthusiasts wanting to communicate complex ideas effectively
  • Aspiring writers and journalists specializing in science
  • Science students and researchers wanting to publish and present their work
  • Communication and media students focusing on science and technology

Relevant academic background

  • Science and technology — biology, chemistry, physics, engineering, computer science
  • Journalism and communication, wishing to specialize in science reporting
  • Interdisciplinary studies bridging science with social sciences, humanities, or arts

Professional experience & aspirations

  • Scientists and researchers who need to publish and communicate findings
  • Science journalists and writers, current or aspiring
  • Educators and communicators doing public outreach
  • Policy makers and advocates in science policy

Personal attributes & skills

  • Strong writing skills, technical and creative
  • Curiosity and passion for science
  • Analytical thinking to break down complex concepts
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Attention to detail, particularly with scientific data

Career aspirations

  • Science communication specialist across print, digital, and broadcast
  • Content creator or blogger focused on science
  • Public relations and outreach coordinator
  • Technical writer for scientific and technical products
