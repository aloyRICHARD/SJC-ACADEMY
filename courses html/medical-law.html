Overview

Course overview

This course provides an in-depth exploration of the legal principles and issues that govern the healthcare industry. It covers the history and sources of medical law, patient rights, healthcare policies, medical malpractice, ethical issues, and the regulation of pharmaceuticals and medical devices.

By the end

Learning objectives

Understand the foundational concepts and sources of medical law.
Analyze laws and regulations governing patient rights and confidentiality.
Evaluate legal issues related to medical malpractice and healthcare policies.
Discuss ethical dilemmas and regulatory frameworks in medical practice.
