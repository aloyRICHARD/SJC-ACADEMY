Practical tools for healthcare professionals to recognize, manage, and mitigate burnout — covering individual stress management, resilience, supportive work culture, organizational change, and mindfulness.

Overview

Course overview

This course is designed to equip healthcare professionals with the knowledge, tools, and strategies to manage and mitigate burnout and stress. The curriculum covers the psychological, physical, and organizational aspects of stress in the healthcare environment, providing practical approaches to fostering resilience, self-care, and a supportive work culture.

Course flow

How the 8 modules progress

The course moves from understanding and assessing burnout, into individual stress-management skills, resilience and self-care, then into building supportive teams and organizations, mindfulness practice, and a capstone project.
