Clinical Dermatology in Practice

A short course for African healthcare settings — recognizing, diagnosing, and treating common and public-health-significant skin conditions with locally available resources.

By the end

Course objectives

Recognize and describe common dermatological conditions prevalent in African communities.
Apply clinical reasoning to diagnose dermatologic diseases using visual and descriptive signs.
Formulate appropriate treatment plans using locally available medications and resources.
Identify skin diseases with public health significance, such as leprosy and fungal infections.
Integrate patient education and counseling into dermatological care.
Course flow

How the 10 modules progress

The course moves from foundations to infectious, then non-infectious disease, into public-health and emergency dermatology, then into practice: diagnostics, local-resource treatment, counseling, and final assessment.
