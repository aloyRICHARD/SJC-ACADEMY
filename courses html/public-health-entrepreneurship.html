Overview

Course overview

This course introduces the concept of entrepreneurship within the public health sector. It explores how innovative ideas can be developed into sustainable ventures that address public health challenges. Students will learn to identify opportunities, create business plans, and understand the unique aspects of entrepreneurship in public health.

By the end

Course objectives

Understand the principles of entrepreneurship and how they apply to public health.
Identify public health challenges that can be addressed through entrepreneurial solutions.
Develop a comprehensive business plan for a public health venture.
Analyze funding options and financial management strategies for public health startups.
Evaluate the impact and scalability of public health innovations.

Who it's for

The ideal student

The ideal student for studying Public Health Entrepreneurship has the academic and practical background in public health and entrepreneurship, plus the passion, skills, and commitment to drive innovative solutions that address public health challenges.

Academic background

  • Public health — epidemiology, health policy, and health systems
  • Business or entrepreneurship — basic business concepts or social innovation

Skills

  • Innovative thinking to develop novel solutions
  • Analytical skills for market, financial, and impact data
  • Research skills to develop viable business plans
  • Communication skills for pitching and engaging stakeholders
  • Project management within timelines and budgets

Personal attributes

  • Passion for improving public health outcomes
  • Entrepreneurial spirit and willingness to take risks
  • Empathy and social awareness for underserved populations
  • Resilience and perseverance

Relevant experience

  • Internship or work experience in public health, healthcare, business development, or social enterprises
  • Involvement in innovation challenges, hackathons, or entrepreneurship clubs
  • Volunteer work in community service or public health initiatives

Academic & career goals

  • Aspiration to start a public health venture, join a startup, or work in health innovation
  • Commitment to solutions that improve health equity

Potential student profiles

  • MPH graduate student interested in health innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Business or MBA student focused on social entrepreneurship or healthcare management
  • Healthcare professional transitioning into innovation and entrepreneurship roles
  • Social science researcher with a passion for entrepreneurial solutions
