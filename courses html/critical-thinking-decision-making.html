Overview

Course overview

This course provides a comprehensive framework for enhancing critical thinking and decision-making skills. Students will learn to apply logical reasoning, assess arguments, solve problems, and make informed decisions in personal and professional contexts. The course combines theoretical foundations with practical exercises to develop effective critical thinking and decision-making strategies.

The course is divided into ten modules, each consisting of lectures, readings, discussions, and assignments. A final project will involve applying critical thinking and decision-making skills to a real-world problem.

By the end

Course objectives

Understand the principles of critical thinking and decision-making.
Identify and evaluate arguments and logical fallacies.
Apply critical thinking skills to solve problems and make decisions.
Use decision-making frameworks and tools effectively.
Reflect on personal decision-making processes and biases.
Course flow

How the 10 modules progress

The course moves from what critical thinking is, into argument evaluation, fallacies and bias, problem-solving and decision frameworks, then group dynamics and emotional intelligence, closing with reflective practice and a final applied project.
