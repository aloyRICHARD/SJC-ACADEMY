Overview

Course overview

"Entrepreneurial Leadership in Africa" is a comprehensive online course designed to equip professionals from various backgrounds with the knowledge, skills, and strategies needed to thrive as entrepreneurs in the dynamic and diverse markets of Africa. This course combines theoretical insights, practical case studies, and interactive exercises to prepare participants for the challenges and opportunities of entrepreneurship across different sectors in Africa.

By the end

Course objectives

Gain an understanding of the entrepreneurial ecosystem in Africa, including market trends, cultural nuances, and regulatory environments.
Identify and evaluate business opportunities across diverse sectors in African markets.
Develop effective leadership and management strategies for entrepreneurial ventures in Africa.
Acquire practical skills in business planning, financing, marketing, and operations tailored to African contexts.
Foster a mindset of innovation, resilience, and social impact to drive sustainable entrepreneurship in Africa.

Participants will apply their learning to develop a comprehensive business plan for a venture in an African market of their choice. They will integrate insights from all course modules to create a strategic roadmap for their entrepreneurial endeavor.
