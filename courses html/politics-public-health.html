Overview

Course overview

This course explores the intersection of politics and public health, examining how political decisions, policies, and frameworks influence public health outcomes. Students will gain an understanding of the political processes that shape health policy and the impact of these policies on health disparities, access to care, and health equity.

By the end

Course objectives

Understand the relationship between politics and public health.
Analyze how political ideologies and systems influence public health policies and outcomes.
Evaluate the impact of health policies on different populations.
Critically assess the role of public health advocacy and activism.
Develop strategies for engaging with political processes to influence public health outcomes.

Who it's for

The ideal student

The best student for this course possesses a blend of academic interests, skills, and personal attributes that align with the interdisciplinary nature of the course.

Academic background

  • Public health knowledge — epidemiology, health policy, and health systems
  • Political science knowledge — political systems, ideologies, and policy-making processes

Skills

  • Analytical skills to critically analyze policies and their public health impacts
  • Research skills — literature reviews, databases, synthesizing sources
  • Communication skills for arguments, policy briefs, and debates
  • Advocacy skills for influencing public health policy

Personal attributes

  • Passion for public health and politics, and a desire to improve health equity
  • Curiosity and open-mindedness across interdisciplinary perspectives
  • Empathy and social awareness for health disparities
  • A problem-solving orientation

Relevant experience

  • Internship or work experience in public health, policy analysis, or related fields
  • Involvement in advocacy — campaigns, political activism, or community service

Academic & career goals

  • Interest in working in public health policy, government agencies, non-profits, or international organizations
  • Commitment to promoting health equity for marginalized populations

Potential student profiles

  • MPH graduate student concentrating in health policy or health administration
  • Political science undergraduate with a strong interest in public health
  • Healthcare professional transitioning into policy and advocacy roles
  • Social science researcher focused on health disparities and public policy
