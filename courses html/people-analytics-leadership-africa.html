Who it's for

The ideal student

The best student for this course blends leadership aspirations with a background in business, HR, or data analytics, and a keen interest in applying data-driven strategies to leadership in the African context — analytically minded, culturally sensitive, and motivated to drive positive organizational change.

Professional interests & goals

  • Aspiring or current leaders wanting to lead diverse teams in Africa
  • HR professionals wanting to leverage data for workforce management
  • Business managers applying analytics to team performance
  • Consultants advising companies operating in Africa

Relevant academic background

  • Business administration — leadership, management, or organizational behavior
  • Human resources management — workforce analytics and talent management
  • Data science and analytics, applied to HR and leadership
  • Social sciences — sociology, psychology, or related fields

Personal attributes & skills

  • Analytical thinking to interpret and use data in decisions
  • Cultural sensitivity across Africa's diverse contexts
  • Excellent communication skills to convey insights and lead teams
  • Strategic vision for long-term organizational goals

Professional experience

  • Prior work experience in African countries or with African teams
  • Leadership or managerial roles impacting team dynamics and performance
  • Any exposure to data-driven decision-making in HR or management

Career aspirations

  • Leadership development specific to the African context
  • HR innovation — data-driven approaches in African organizations
  • Organizational consultancy specializing in effectiveness and leadership through analytics
