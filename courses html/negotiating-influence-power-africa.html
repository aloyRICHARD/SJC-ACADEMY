Overview

Course overview

This course explores the dynamics of influence and power in the African context, focusing on political, economic, and social spheres. It aims to provide students with an understanding of the unique challenges and opportunities in negotiating and applying influence and power across various sectors in Africa. Students will gain practical skills in negotiation, strategic thinking, and leadership within the African geopolitical landscape.

By the end

Course objectives

Understand the historical and contemporary contexts of power and influence in Africa.
Analyze the key players and structures that shape power dynamics in Africa.
Develop negotiation strategies tailored to the African context.
Apply principles of influence and power in real-world scenarios.
Critically assess the impact of external actors on African political and economic systems.
Course flow

How the 10 modules progress

The course moves from historical and political power, through economic and social-cultural influence, into negotiation and strategic leadership, then external actors, conflict resolution, and sustainable development — closing with a final strategic plan.
