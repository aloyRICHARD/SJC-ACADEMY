Who We Are

The training ground behind published journals.

SJC Academy is the capacity-building arm of SJC Publishers Company Limited, Africa's most trusted and distinguished publishing house. Where SJC connects African research to a global audience, SJC Academy prepares the researchers themselves.

Many talented researchers don't struggle because their ideas lack merit — they struggle from limited access to structured training in methodology, data analysis, or academic writing. SJC Academy closes that gap, drawing on the same editorial standards and academic networks that make SJC a trusted publishing name.

Our Mission

To empower scholars, researchers, and professionals with the knowledge, tools, and confidence to conduct rigorous research and share it with the world.

Our Vision

To become the leading capacity-building platform for researchers in Africa and beyond, turning promising ideas into publishable, high-impact scholarship.

Our Curriculum

Six tracks, one goal: publishable research.

Each track is built to strengthen a specific stage of the research journey, from first hypothesis to final submission.

01

Research Methodology Training

Quantitative methods including statistical analysis and hypothesis testing, alongside qualitative methods such as ethnography, case studies, and thematic analysis.

02

Data Analysis Workshops

Hands-on proficiency in SPSS, R, and NVivo, plus advanced techniques like multivariate analysis, machine learning, and data visualization.

03

Academic Writing & Publishing

Manuscript structure and preparation, journal selection strategy, the peer-review process, and how to respond constructively to reviewer feedback.

04

Grant Writing & Funding

Building compelling proposals with clear objectives and methodology, and navigating funding sources across disciplines.

05

Ethical Research Practices

Standards for consent, data privacy, and responsible conduct — embedding integrity and accountability into every study.

06

Collaborative Research & Networking

Interdisciplinary collaboration through workshops and seminars that expand each researcher's academic network.
